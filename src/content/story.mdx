Surprise! Emily Rose Davis and Leif Emmanuel Hetland eloped on Wednesday, May 20, 2026. After 13 years together, we hosted an intimate, symbolic ceremony in Birmingham, AL on May 16, 2026.

Emily’s father, Eugene Moor Davis (“Moe”) officiated the ceremony, joined by our mothers Nancy Davis and Jennifer Hetland, brother Will Davis and his wife Anna Grace, Leif’s sister Katherine Hetland, and our nephew Malachi Ray. Due to the spontaneity of the occasion, Leif’s father Leif H. Hetland, sisters Laila Hetland and Courtney Barmala-Hetland alongside her husband Vegard and daughters Rebekka and Rosie were not able to attend the ceremony, but supported from afar and were there in our hearts.

The ceremony was followed by an intimate reception at the Davis’ home. Nancy provided the venue and decor, Jennifer made the wedding cake and desserts, Leif provided the catering [consider one more word here — e.g. “handled catering” or “cooked for the reception”], Katherine provided photography and floral arrangements, and Will and Anna Grace helped with the infrastructure and clean up. The bride contributed her own flower crown, a nod to Scandinavian traditions. She also wore her paternal grandmother’s diamond necklace and an add-a-pearl necklace that her Aunt Sharon had been assembling Emily’s entire life, for her to wear on her wedding day. Emily’s engagement ring is an heirloom piece, passed down from the Hetland family.

Upon returning home to Atlanta, we went to the Fulton County Clerk’s Office on May 19 for our marriage license. On May 20, our marriage was formally documented in the backyard of our home by the honorable Judge Rashida Oliver. This step felt extra special as we were able to include our home and cat, Bunz, in the official citation.

We celebrated with a delicious dinner at Houston’s, a beloved Hetland family establishment, followed by dessert and a movie at home.

Although we wish our entire community of family and friends could have been with us physically, you all were there in spirit and in our hearts. The day felt very “us,” and we are so proud of what we accomplished in a short period of time. Since he is battling advanced cancer, we feel so blessed that Emily’s father, Moe, was able to conduct our ceremony and bless our union.

To keep with the trend of non-traditional, we hope to continue the celebrations throughout 2026 and beyond. We hope to celebrate with our American family and friends whenever we can see you all and get dates on the books. We also want to get back to Norway so we can celebrate with Leif’s paternal side of the family.

We hope you all know how much you mean to us. If this website made it to you, we love you, and we thank you for being a part of our story! Cheers and ❤️ from Emily and Leif Hetland!!!
