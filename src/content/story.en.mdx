Surprise! After over 13 years together, Emily Rose Davis and Leif Emmanuel Hetland are married.

Our celebration unfolded across several days. On May 16, 2026, we hosted an intimate, symbolic ceremony in Birmingham, AL, officiated by Emily's father, Moe, who is battling cancer. Having him officiate the wedding meant everything to us.

Joining us were our mothers Nancy and Jennifer, brother Will and his wife Anna Grace, Leif's sister Katherine, and our nephew Malachi Ray. Due to the spontaneity of the occasion, Leif's father Leif H., along with sisters Laila and Courtney, her husband Vegard, and their daughters Rebekka and Rosalyn, were not able to attend in person, but supported us and were felt from afar.

The ceremony was followed by an intimate reception at the Davis' home. Nancy provided the venue and decor, Jennifer made the wedding cake and desserts, Leif handled the catering, Katherine provided photography and floral arrangements, Will and Anna Grace helped with the infrastructure and clean up. The bride contributed her own flower crown, a nod to Scandinavian traditions. She also wore her paternal grandmother's diamond necklace and an "add-a-pearl" necklace that her Aunt Sharon had been assembling for her to wear on her wedding day. Emily's engagement ring is an heirloom piece, passed down from the Hetland family.

Upon returning home to Atlanta, we went to the Fulton County Courthouse on May 19 for our marriage license. On May 20, our marriage was formally documented in the backyard of our home by the honorable Judge Rashida Oliver. This step felt extra special, as we were able to include our home and cat, Bunz, in the official record. We celebrated with dinner at Houston's, a longtime Hetland family favorite, followed by dessert and a movie at home.

Although we wish our entire community of family and friends could have been with us physically, you all were there in spirit and in our hearts. The day felt very "us," and we are so proud of what we accomplished in a short period of time.

In keeping with our non-traditional streak, we hope to continue the celebrations throughout 2026 and beyond, with our family and friends whenever we can get dates on the books, and a trip back to Norway to celebrate with Leif's paternal side of the family.

We hope you all know how much you mean to us. If this website made it to you, we love you, and we thank you for being a part of our story!

Cheers and ❤️
Emily and Leif Hetland (and Bunz 🐱)!!!
